## Project Overview

This project focuses on using Power BI to explore brain tumour data and identify patterns across patient characteristics and tumour distributions.

## Dataset

The dataset contains patient-level brain tumour records, including demographic information, tumour characteristics, affected brain regions and diagnosis details.

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
|---|---|
| **Key fields** | Age, Gender, Tumour Type, Tumour Grade, Brain Region, Tumour Size, Diagnosis Year |

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared in Power Query to ensure the data was suitable for analysis.

Key steps included:

- Reviewed data types and field structure
- Checked missing values and inconsistencies
- Standardised categorical fields
- Cleaned demographic and tumour-related fields
- Prepared fields for DAX calculations

## Key Analysis Areas

**Patient Demographics**  
Analysed patient distribution across age groups.

**Tumour Type**  
Compared the distribution of recorded tumour types.

**Tumour Grade**  
Examined case distribution across tumour grades.

**Brain Region**  
Compared tumour occurrence across affected brain regions.

**Diagnosis Trends**  
Analysed changes in recorded diagnoses across the available years.

## Key Insights

- Tumour distribution varied across tumour types, with some categories accounting for a larger share of recorded cases.
- Case counts differed across brain regions, highlighting variation in recorded tumour locations.
- Patient distribution varied across age groups, with the 60+ group representing the largest recorded age group.
- Tumour grades were unevenly distributed, with some grades appearing more frequently than others.
- Diagnosis volumes changed over time, indicating variation in recorded cases across the study period.

> **Note:** Findings describe patterns within the dataset and should not be interpreted as clinical conclusions.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Segment patient data** by age group to support more detailed analysis.
2. **Compare tumour types and grades** to identify areas for further investigation.
3. **Monitor brain-region distributions** to identify notable concentrations in recorded cases.
4. **Track diagnosis trends over time** to identify changes in recorded case volumes.
5. **Use interactive filtering** to investigate specific combinations of patient and tumour characteristics.
